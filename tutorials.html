BASE44

How to Build an App with Base44

Once you have your Base44 account, here's how to build your first web app:

1

Plan Your App

Before touching the builder, answer: What problem does your app solve? Who is your target user? What are the 3 core features? What data do you need to store?

2

Create Your Data Model

Go to the Data section and create your tables. For a task app: Users (auto-created), Projects (title, status, owner), Tasks (title, project, assignee, status).

3

Design Your UI

Use the visual builder to create: a Dashboard, List views (tables/cards), and Detail/Edit views. Base44 handles login/signup automatically.

4

Add Your First Feature

Connect UI to data: create a form to add records, display records in a list, add edit/delete buttons, and test with real data.

5

Publish & Share

Click Publish, get your unique app URL, share it with users, and iterate based on feedback.

WIX

How to Build a Website with Wix

Wix makes it easy to launch a professional website without writing code. Here's how:

1

Choose Your Template

Pick a template that matches your industry — restaurant, portfolio, blog, store, or service business. You can switch templates later.

2

Customize Your Content

Replace placeholder text with your own: business name, tagline, services, pricing, and contact info. Upload your logo and brand colors.

3

Add Pages & Features

Add pages like About, Contact, Blog, or Shop. Wix includes built-in tools for bookings, email marketing, SEO, and analytics.

4

Set Up Your Domain

Connect a custom domain or use a free wixsite.com address. Premium plans include a free domain for the first year.

5

Publish Your Site

Click Publish and your site is live. Connect Google Analytics, submit to search engines, and start sharing your URL.
