STEP 2

How to Build Your First App

Once you have your Base44 account, here's how to build your first web app from scratch:

1

Plan Your App

Before touching the builder, sketch out what you want to build. Answer these questions:

  • What problem does your app solve?
  • Who is your target user?
  • What are the 3 core features?
  • What data do you need to store?
2

Create Your Data Model

In Base44, go to the Data section and create your tables. For example, a simple task app might need:

Users (auto-created)
├── email
└── name

Projects
├── title (text)
├── status (select)
└── owner (→ Users)

Tasks
├── title (text)
├── project (→ Projects)
└── status (select)
3

Design Your UI

Use Base44's visual builder to create your pages. Start with:

  • Login/Signup page — Base44 handles this automatically
  • Dashboard — Your main landing page after login
  • List views — Show your data in tables or cards
  • Detail/Edit views — Create and edit records
4

Add Your First Feature

Connect your UI to your data. Start simple:

  • Create a form to add records
  • Display records in a list
  • Add edit/delete buttons
  • Test with real data
5

Publish & Share

When you're ready:

  • Click Publish in Base44
  • Get your unique app URL
  • Share it with users or customers
  • Iterate based on feedback
