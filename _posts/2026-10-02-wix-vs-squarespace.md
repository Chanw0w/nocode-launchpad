## Ease of Use

### Wix

Wix's editor is free-form — you can drag elements anywhere on the page. This gives you freedom but can lead to inconsistent layouts if you're not careful.

The ADI (Artificial Design Intelligence) feature can auto-generate a site based on your answers. It's a good starting point for absolute beginners.

### Squarespace

Squarespace uses a structured editor — elements snap to a grid. This keeps your design clean and consistent but gives you less freedom.

The editor is slightly harder to learn but produces more professional results by default.

## Templates

### Wix: 800+ Templates

Wix has one of the largest template libraries. Templates are organized by industry — restaurants, portfolios, stores, services, and more.

**Downside:** Once you choose a template and publish, you can't switch. Choose carefully.

### Squarespace: 100+ Templates

Squarespace has fewer templates but they're more polished. Every template looks professional out of the box.

**Upside:** You can switch templates after publishing (on the old version — check current policy).

## Blogging

Squarespace wins for blogging. Its blog layouts are more refined, the editor is better for long-form content, and the category/tag system is more flexible.

Wix blogging is functional but basic. If blogging is central to your site, Squarespace is the better choice.

## FAQ

### Is Wix better than Squarespace?
Wix is easier to use and has more templates. Squarespace has more polished designs and better for creative portfolios. Neither is objectively better — it depends on your priorities.

### Which is cheaper, Wix or Squarespace?
Wix has a free plan (Squarespace doesn't). Paid plans are similar: Wix starts at $17/mo, Squarespace at $16/mo. For e-commerce, both charge around $27-33/mo.

### Can I switch from Wix to Squarespace?
There's no direct migration. You'd rebuild your site on the new platform. Content can be exported/imported in some cases, but design and structure need to be recreated.

### Which has better templates, Wix or Squarespace?
Wix has more templates (800+ vs 100+). Squarespace templates are more polished and cohesive. If you want quantity and variety, choose Wix. If you want refined design quality, choose Squarespace.

### Is Squarespace better for portfolios?
Yes, Squarespace is generally better for creative portfolios. Its templates are designed with photographers, designers, and artists in mind. The layouts are more gallery-focused and visually refined.
