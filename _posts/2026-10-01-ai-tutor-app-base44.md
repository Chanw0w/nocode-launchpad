## Why Build an AI Tutor?

Most tutoring apps just give you the answer. That's not how learning works. The **Socratic method** — asking guiding questions instead of providing answers — has been proven to help students understand concepts deeper and retain information longer.

I wanted to build an app that uses this approach, powered by AI. And I wanted to build it without writing a single line of code.

## What I Built

**Thinkerwell** — a personalized AI tutoring app that:

- Uses the **Socratic method** to guide students to answers through questions
- Adapts to each student's **grade level** and **preferred learning style**
- Tracks **streaks, progress, and mastery** across subjects
- Supports multiple subjects: math, science, history, programming, and more
- Includes a **courses dashboard** where students can browse and track their learning path

The app features a friendly **robot owl mascot** — a wise tutor character that guides students through their learning journey. The owl appears on the home page, onboarding flow, and quiz sessions, giving the app a warm, approachable feel.

### How the Socratic Method Works in the App

Instead of saying "The answer is 42," the tutor asks:

1. **"What do you already know about this problem?"** — Gets the student thinking
2. **"What would happen if we tried X?"** — Guides exploration
3. **"Can you see a pattern here?"** — Helps discover the solution
4. **"Why do you think that works?"** — Reinforces understanding

The student arrives at the answer themselves — which means they actually understand it.
