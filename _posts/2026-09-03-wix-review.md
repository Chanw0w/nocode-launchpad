Wix powers over 200 million websites. I've used it for client projects and personal sites — here's my honest take on what it does well and where it falls short.

## What Is Wix?

Wix is a drag-and-drop website builder for creating professional websites without coding. It includes 800+ templates, built-in SEO tools, e-commerce, and an app market for extending functionality.

Unlike app builders like Base44 or Bubble, Wix is designed for **websites** — portfolios, business sites, blogs, and online stores. Not web applications with user accounts and databases.

## Who Is Wix For?

**Good fit if you're a:**
- Small business owner who needs a professional website
- Freelancer building a portfolio
- Restaurant, salon, or local service business
- Blogger who wants simplicity over customization
- Anyone who values ease of use over maximum control

**Not ideal if you need:**
- A web application with user accounts (use Base44)
- Pixel-perfect design control (use Webflow)
- A blog with thousands of posts (use WordPress)
- Maximum SEO control (use WordPress)

## Key Features

### 800+ Templates

Wix has one of the largest template libraries. Every template is mobile-responsive and designed for specific industries — restaurants, portfolios, stores, services, and more.

### Drag-and-Drop Editor

The editor lets you place elements anywhere on the page. Unlike some builders that restrict layout, Wix gives you near-total freedom. This is great for beginners but can lead to inconsistent designs if you're not careful.

### Wix ADI (Artificial Design Intelligence)

Wix can auto-generate a website based on your answers to a few questions. It's not perfect, but it gives you a starting point that you can customize.

### App Market

250+ apps for adding functionality — bookings, live chat, social media feeds, forms, and more. Most are free or low-cost.

### E-commerce

Wix Business plans include a full online store with payment processing, shipping, inventory management, and abandoned cart recovery.

### SEO Tools

Customizable meta tags, URL structures, sitemaps, 301 redirects, and structured data. Wix SEO has improved significantly — it's now competitive for most small business needs.

## FAQ

### Is Wix really free?
Yes, Wix has a free plan that lets you build and publish a website. The free plan includes Wix branding and a Wix subdomain (yourname.wixsite.com). Paid plans remove branding and add custom domains.

### Is Wix good for SEO?
Wix has improved its SEO tools significantly. It offers customizable meta tags, URL structures, sitemaps, and 301 redirects. For most small business websites, Wix SEO is adequate. Advanced users may prefer WordPress.

### Can I sell products with Wix?
Yes, Wix has built-in e-commerce on its Business plans. You can sell physical products, digital goods, and services with payment processing, shipping, and inventory management.

### Wix vs Squarespace — which is better?
Wix is easier to use with more templates and a free plan. Squarespace has more polished designs and better for creative portfolios. Choose Wix for flexibility, Squarespace for aesthetics.

### Can I change my Wix template after publishing?
No, Wix doesn't let you switch templates after your site is live. You'd need to create a new site with a different template. Choose your template carefully before publishing.

### How much does Wix cost?
Wix has a free plan. Paid plans start at $17/month (Light) for custom domains and go up to $159/month (Business Elite) for advanced e-commerce. Most small businesses need the $29/month Core plan.
