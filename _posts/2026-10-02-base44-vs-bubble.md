## Ease of Use

### Base44

Base44's biggest strength is simplicity. The AI assistant lets you describe features in plain English and generates the data models and UI for you. I built a working Client Portal in an afternoon.

The learning curve is minimal. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can use Base44.

### Bubble

Bubble is more powerful but significantly harder to learn. The visual editor has hundreds of options, and concepts like workflows, reusable elements, and data types take time to master.

Expect 2-4 weeks to feel comfortable with Bubble vs. 2-4 hours with Base44.

## When to Choose Base44

- You're a non-technical founder building your first MVP
- You want AI to help scaffold your app
- Speed matters more than deep customization
- You're building a SaaS tool, internal dashboard, or client portal
- Budget is tight (free tier is generous)

## When to Choose Bubble

- You need complex multi-step workflows
- You're building a marketplace or social platform
- You need access to a large plugin ecosystem
- You're willing to invest weeks learning the platform
- You need features that go beyond standard CRUD apps

## Performance

Both platforms host your app on their infrastructure. In my experience:

- **Base44**: Fast for standard apps. Occasional lag on data-heavy pages.
- **Bubble**: Can be slow if you don't optimize. Requires more attention to performance.

For apps with under 10,000 users, both perform adequately. For high-traffic applications, neither matches custom-coded solutions.

## Switching Between Platforms

There's no migration tool between Base44 and Bubble. If you start on one and need to switch, you'll rebuild from scratch. This makes your initial choice important.

**My recommendation:** Start with Base44 for your MVP. If you outgrow it and need Bubble's advanced features, you'll have validated your idea and can justify the rebuild cost.

## FAQ

### Is Base44 better than Bubble?
Base44 is better for beginners who want to build apps quickly with AI assistance. Bubble is better for complex applications that need deep customization. Neither is objectively better — it depends on your project and technical comfort.

### Can I switch from Bubble to Base44?
There's no direct migration tool. You'd need to rebuild your app on Base44 from scratch. If you're early in your project, switching is feasible. For established apps, the rebuild cost may not be worth it.

### Which is cheaper, Base44 or Bubble?
Both have free tiers. Base44's paid plans start at around $19/month while Bubble starts at $29/month. For simple apps, Base44 is more affordable. For complex apps, the price difference matters less than feature fit.

### Can I build a marketplace with Base44 or Bubble?
Bubble is better suited for marketplaces due to its more mature workflow engine and plugin ecosystem. Base44 can handle simpler marketplace concepts but may lack the depth needed for complex multi-sided platforms.

### Which has better AI features, Base44 or Bubble?
Base44 has built-in AI assistance that helps you generate app features by describing what you want. Bubble has AI plugins but they're not native to the platform. For AI-first development, Base44 is the stronger choice.
