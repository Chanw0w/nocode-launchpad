## What I Built

I created a **Business Idea Validator** — a guided tool that walks aspiring entrepreneurs through validating their business idea before they invest time or money.

### How It Works

The validator takes users through 6 steps:

**Step 1: Describe Your Idea**
- Free-text input: "What does your business do?"
- AI helps clarify vague descriptions

**Step 2: Define Your Target Audience**
- Who is this for?
- What problem does it solve for them?
- How urgent is the problem?

**Step 3: Identify Competitors**
- List 3 competitors
- If stuck, AI suggests competitors based on your description
- Analyze their strengths and weaknesses

**Step 4: Score Your Idea**
The validator scores your idea across 5 dimensions:
- **Market size** (1-10): Is the market big enough?
- **Competition** (1-10): How crowded is the space?
- **Feasibility** (1-10): Can you actually build this?
- **Timing** (1-10): Is the market ready for this?
- **Differentiation** (1-10): What makes you unique?

**Step 5: Get Your Validation Report**
Based on your scores, the validator generates:
- An overall validation score (out of 100)
- Strengths and weaknesses analysis
- Key risks to address
- Recommendations for next steps

**Step 6: Take Action**
- If validated: "Build your MVP with Base44 →"
- If not validated: "Here's what to fix before building"

### What Makes This Powerful

The validator doesn't just give you a score. It:
- Uses AI to analyze your idea against market conditions
- Suggests competitors you might have missed
- Identifies blind spots in your thinking
- Provides actionable next steps
