## 1. Wix — Best Overall

**Why it's #1:** Wix offers the best combination of ease of use, features, and value. The 800+ templates mean you'll find something that fits your industry, and the drag-and-drop editor is the most intuitive available.

**Best for:** Small businesses, freelancers, restaurants, portfolios, blogs

**Pros:** Free plan, 800+ templates, easiest editor, app market, built-in SEO tools

**Cons:** Can't switch templates after publishing, free plan has branding, site speed can vary

## 2. Squarespace — Best for Design

Squarespace templates are the most polished in the industry. If design quality matters more than template variety, Squarespace is the choice.

**Best for:** Creative portfolios, photographers, designers, artists

**Pros:** Beautiful templates, clean code, faster loading, better blogging

**Cons:** No free plan, fewer templates, slightly harder to learn

[Wix vs Squarespace comparison →](/nocode-launchpad/wix-vs-squarespace/)

## 3. WordPress — Most Flexible

WordPress powers 40%+ of the web. It's the most flexible option but requires more technical knowledge — you'll manage hosting, updates, and security yourself.

**Best for:** Blogs, complex sites, advanced SEO, custom functionality

**Pros:** Unlimited flexibility, 50,000+ plugins, best SEO, largest community

**Cons:** Steep learning curve, requires maintenance, hosting costs extra

## 4. Webflow — Best for Designers

Webflow gives designers pixel-perfect control without writing code. It's more powerful than Wix but harder to learn.

**Best for:** Designers, agencies, marketing sites, portfolios

**Pros:** Pixel-perfect control, clean code export, built-in CMS, animations

**Cons:** Steeper learning curve, no free plan on custom domains, more expensive

## 5. Shopify — Best for E-commerce

If you're building an online store, Shopify is the specialist. It handles products, payments, shipping, and inventory better than any general-purpose builder.

**Best for:** Online stores, dropshipping, e-commerce businesses

**Pros:** Best e-commerce features, 6,000+ apps, payment processing, shipping

**Cons:** More expensive ($29/mo+), transaction fees on non-Shopify payments, not for non-store sites

## 6. Carrd — Best for Simple Sites

Carrd builds beautiful one-page sites for $9/year. If you just need a landing page, link-in-bio, or simple portfolio, Carrd is the cheapest option.

**Best for:** Landing pages, link-in-bio sites, simple portfolios

**Pros:** Incredibly cheap ($9/year), beautiful templates, fast setup

**Cons:** One-page only, no blogging, limited functionality

## FAQ

### What is the best website builder in 2026?
Wix is our top pick for most users. It offers 800+ templates, a free plan, and the easiest drag-and-drop editor. For creative portfolios, Squarespace has more polished designs. For maximum flexibility, WordPress.

### Can I build a website for free?
Yes, Wix and WordPress.com both have free plans. Free plans include platform branding and subdomains. Paid plans ($12-30/month) remove branding and add custom domains.

### Which website builder is best for SEO?
WordPress is the best for SEO with plugins like Yoast and Rank Math. Wix and Squarespace have good built-in SEO tools. For most small businesses, any of these platforms handle SEO adequately.

### Should I use WordPress or a website builder?
Use a website builder (Wix, Squarespace) if you want simplicity and don't want to manage hosting, updates, or security. Use WordPress if you need maximum flexibility, advanced SEO, or complex functionality.

### How much does a website cost in 2026?
With a website builder: $0-30/month for most sites. With WordPress: $5-30/month for hosting plus domain ($12/year). Custom development: $3,000-$50,000+. Website builders are the cheapest option for simple sites.
