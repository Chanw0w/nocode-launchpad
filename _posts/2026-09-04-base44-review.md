## What Is Base44?

Base44 is a no-code platform that lets you build full-stack web applications without writing code. You design your UI visually, define your data models, and Base44 handles the backend, database, authentication, and hosting.

Unlike website builders (Wix, Squarespace), Base44 is built for **web applications** — things with user accounts, databases, forms, and logic. Think SaaS tools, internal dashboards, and client portals.

## Who Is Base44 For?

**Good fit if you're a:**
- Non-technical founder validating a SaaS idea
- Freelancer building internal tools for clients
- Small business owner who needs a custom app
- Entrepreneur who wants to test ideas before hiring developers

**Not ideal if you need:**
- A simple blog or portfolio (use Wix or WordPress)
- A complex app with millions of users (hire developers)
- Heavy customization beyond what the visual builder offers
- Mobile-native apps (Base44 is web-only)

## Key Features

### Visual App Builder

Base44's drag-and-drop builder lets you create pages, forms, tables, and dashboards. You connect UI elements to your data models without writing code. The builder includes pre-built components like login forms, data tables, charts, and modals.

### AI-Powered Development

The built-in AI assistant helps you generate app features by describing what you want. Tell it "create a project management dashboard with task assignments" and it scaffolds the data models and UI for you. This is the biggest time-saver.

### Database & Backend

Base44 manages your database automatically. You define tables and relationships visually — no SQL, no server setup. It handles user authentication, file storage, and API endpoints out of the box.

### Hosting & Deployment

Every Base44 app gets a free subdomain (yourapp.base44.app) with SSL. Paid plans let you connect custom domains. Deployment is one click — no Docker, no CI/CD, no DevOps.
