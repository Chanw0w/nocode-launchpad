## Design Principles Applied

### 1. Visual Hierarchy
- Bold focal points with clear subject separation
- Depth of field to guide attention
- Negative space for premium feel

### 2. Motion Design
- Purposeful animations that tell a story
- Satisfying transformations (problem → solution)
- Smooth, cinematic transitions

### 3. Color Psychology
- **Base44 campaigns:** Warm gradients (purple-pink-orange), confident greens
- **Wix campaigns:** Clean whites, creative energy
- **Problem scenes:** Red/amber → Green transformation

### 4. Premium Aesthetic
- Soft lighting, depth, particles
- Minimal clutter, focused messaging
- High-end SaaS/product showcase feel
